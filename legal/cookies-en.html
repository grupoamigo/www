Cookie Policy

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website saves in your browser to remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language or your consent choices. This policy explains which cookies the site www.grupoamigo.com, operated by Almacenajes y Maniobras Integrales del Golfo, S.A. de C.V. ("Grupo Amigo"), uses and how you can manage them.

This policy complements our Privacy Notice and is governed by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the ePrivacy Directive, and, in Mexico, the LFPDPPP.

2. Legal basis and consent

We only load non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising) after obtaining your explicit consent through the banner shown when you arrive at the site. Until you decide, all optional categories remain disabled via Google Consent Mode v2. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time.

4. Other services without cookies

To reduce the impact on your privacy, web fonts are served via bunny.net Fonts, a European service (Slovenia) that does not set cookies, does not keep logs and does not collect identifiers from visitors. For this reason it does not require prior consent, but we disclose it for transparency.

5. International transfers

Some providers (Google) may process data outside the European Economic Area. These transfers are made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

6. How to manage or withdraw your consent

  • Click at any time.
  • Clear the site's storage in your browser to make the banner appear again.
  • Configure your browser to block third-party cookies.
